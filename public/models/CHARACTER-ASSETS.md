# Station character assets

These public-domain CC0 character models are used for the station crowd. The local GLBs were reduced for real-time web rendering.

- `station-woman-walk.glb` — Maya, a rigged adult woman with a relaxed walk cycle. Source: [Innerscene 3D Parts Library](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/relaxed-walk-maya-3d-person-animated-2c6669a2).
- `station-family.glb` — Sam, Mina, Eli, and Riley, a multi-age family group. Source: [Innerscene 3D Parts Library](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/sam-mina-eli-and-riley-3d-person-family-of-four-7d1199a8).
- `station-talking-group.glb` — Micah, Taylor, and Quinn, posed as a conversation group. Source: [Innerscene 3D Parts Library](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/micah-taylor-and-quinn-3d-person-three-colleagues-talking-4bdae510).

The existing `station-passenger.glb` remains the adult male walking character used for the older passenger and boarding animation.
